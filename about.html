About me

EEE student at Daffodil International University, teaching assistant and IEEE volunteer.

I study Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Daffodil International University, and most of what I know I learned at the bench: simulating a circuit, laying out the board, writing the firmware, then finding out what I got wrong. My favourite build so far is ProtectA, an ESP32-S3 box that trips on over-voltage, over-current and earth leakage, then recloses on its own.

Outside the lab I spend a lot of time with IEEE. I chaired the Power & Energy Society chapter at DIU, I'm now Vice-Chair (Technical) of the student branch, and as a Teaching Assistant I help 100+ students through circuits, power electronics and digital electronics labs.
